\begin{key}[The bottleneck principle (write it in your diagnosis)]
$T \ge \max(\text{compute},\ \text{memory bytes}/\text{BW},\ \text{critical path},\ \text{serial part}) + T_{comm}+T_{sync}+T_{imbalance}$. The symptom is one term binding. Fix it, then say which term binds next (Lab 8: ``the bottleneck moves'').
\end{key}
